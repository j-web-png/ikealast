Strategic Recommendations

1. Cap Non-Core OpEx

  • Review discretionary spending and eliminate non-essential operational costs.
  • Introduce departmental spending limits and monitor monthly expenditure.
  • Improve operational efficiency without compromising product quality.

2. Vertical Integration

  • Strengthen supplier relationships to improve procurement efficiency.
  • Reduce reliance on external suppliers where economically viable.
  • Improve supply-chain control to reduce material and logistics costs.

3. Improve Production Capacity

  • Invest in modern production equipment and manufacturing technology.
  • Optimise production processes to increase output and reduce waste.
  • Strengthen sales forecasting and demand planning to align production capacity with customer demand.
  • Develop sales growth initiatives to increase revenue by attracting new customers and encouraging repeat purchases.
